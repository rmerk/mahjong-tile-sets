04 — Voice & Tone

Brand Voice

TileSmith speaks with quiet confidence. We're artisans, not salespeople — knowledgeable, warm, and precise. We let the craft do the talking.

Confident, Not Boastful

We know our craft is exceptional. We state it plainly without superlatives or hyperbole.

"Each tile is 3D printed at 600-micron resolution and finished by hand."
"Our AMAZING tiles are the BEST mahjong tiles ever created!!!"

Personal, Not Casual

We connect with our customers as individuals. We're warm but never sloppy.

"Tell us about the place that matters most to you. We'll build a set around it."
"Hey guys! Hit us up with your fave spots and we'll totally hook you up lol"

Specific, Not Vague

Precision builds trust. We name materials, processes, and details.

"PLA+ filament, layer-by-layer. 166 tiles, each one unique to your theme."
"High-quality materials and premium craftsmanship."

Storied, Not Decorative

Every design choice has meaning. We explain the "why" behind the art.

"The 1-Crack features 'You Betcha' — the two words every Minnesotan knows."
"Fun and quirky designs to make your game pop!"

06 — Usage Guidelines

Brand Usage

Rules for maintaining brand consistency across all touchpoints — from packaging to social media to the website.

Do

Use the full "TileSmith" wordmark with the icon. Maintain clear space equal to the icon height on all sides. Always color "Smith" in Forge Gold when on dark or light backgrounds.

Don't

Never separate the "T" icon from the wordmark. Never use the logo smaller than 120px wide. Never place the logo on busy backgrounds without a solid backing shape.

Photography

Product photography should be clean, well-lit, and shot on neutral surfaces (wood, linen, matte paper). Show tiles in context — hands holding them, arranged mid-game, grouped by suit.

Avoid

Avoid cluttered compositions, heavy filters, or neon/saturated backdrops. The tile artwork is the star — everything else should recede. No stock photography of generic game nights.
